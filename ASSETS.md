# Assets

**Art direction:** Neon arcade aesthetic on a deep navy (#0a0a2e) tunnel background. Vibrant saturated gate colors (red, orange, yellow, lime green, cyan, purple) split into 6 equal 60° segments with thin dark borders. Glossy white sphere ball with soft glow. Clean sharp digital game rendering. Gold/cyan accent colors for UI elements (score, multiplier). Subtle neon light streaks along tunnel walls. No motion blur, no depth of field. High-contrast, readable HUD text in bold white.
